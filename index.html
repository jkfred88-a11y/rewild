How to rate SUDS

SUDS is how distressed or afraid you feel, not how hard you're working or what your heart rate is. A pounding heart on a climb with no fear is a low SUDS.

0Completely calm.
25Noticeable, but easy to stay with.
50Clearly uncomfortable; I can keep going.
75Very hard to stay; a strong urge to leave.
100The worst I've ever felt. October 10 on the trail is my reference point.

When to rate each one

  • Predicted: before you go, the peak you expect.
  • Before: right before you start.
  • Peak: the highest point during. A rough memory afterward is fine; don't rate as you go.
  • After: at the end, or within about ten minutes.

Use the same anchors every time so the numbers mean the same thing next month. A high SUDS doesn't make an exposure a failure; it means you did something hard.
